# Generated marketing illustrations

Tool: built-in imagegen. Final images were inspected, converted to WebP and copied into the project. No external image hosting is required.

## public/images/document-studio.webp

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape hero illustration for CoDoc, a Russian online visual document template editor. Premium editorial 3D paper still life: beautifully designed cream certificate with embossed geometric seal, lavender event ticket with perforation and abstract barcode, small white business document with neat dark typographic lines, arranged as an elegant overlapping fan floating above a very pale lavender studio background. Deep violet and lime accents, realistic paper grain, soft daylight and dimensional shadows. Tight cohesive composition, generous breathing room, no people, no software interface, no logos, no watermarks, no legible text. Landscape 3:2.

## public/images/template-series.webp

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: wide website illustration for CoDoc document automation section. Premium studio 3D still life on very pale lavender background: one beautifully designed cream certificate on left, a small neat stack of three matching personalized certificates fanning out on right, and three lavender and lime translucent data tiles flowing from left to right between them. Paper grain, embossed seal, dark violet geometric typography lines, lime accents, tasteful soft natural shadows. No legible text, no letters, no interface, no logos, no people, no watermark. Wide landscape 3:2. Visually communicate one template becoming a series of individual documents, generous uncluttered spacing.
